# HSK 2026 vocabulary sources

- Vocabulary, pinyin, level and part of speech: *HSK考试大纲*, published by the Center for Language Education and Cooperation in November 2025 and effective July 2026.
- English dictionary fields and Japanese glosses: adapted from CC-CEDICT-derived data published by Second Language under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- The 33-word compatibility supplement contains headwords present in the former HSK 2.0 levels 1–3 but absent as standalone headwords from the 2026 list. Its examples and Japanese glosses come from this application's pre-migration vocabulary data.
